# Objection and Revision Protocol

## Objection quality

An objection must identify:

- the exact disputed claim or missing evidence;
- causal mechanism or decision consequence;
- severity and affected requirement/interface;
- supporting and contradicting evidence;
- proposed correction or discriminating test;
- agent responsible for confirming resolution.

“I disagree” and “this may be risky” are not sufficient.

## Resolution paths

### Calculation or simulation

Record inputs, units, assumptions, model version, corners, result and independent check.

### Source verification

Use the authoritative revision and exact section/table. Preserve whether a value is guaranteed, typical, inferred or measured.

### Experiment

State competing hypotheses and predicted observations before execution. Capture configuration, tools, calibration, raw data, processing and uncertainty.

### Proposal change

Create a new proposal version and declare every affected interface. Re-screen all agents and reject old approvals.

### Explicit risk acceptance

Use only when the uncertainty cannot be removed economically and the remaining risk is within authorized limits. Record rationale, containment and revisit trigger.

## Prohibited resolutions

- majority vote;
- deleting the objection;
- paraphrasing the same unsupported claim;
- citing another agent's confidence;
- silently narrowing test conditions;
- mixing evidence from incompatible hardware/firmware/configuration revisions;
- calling an unresolved condition “future work” while releasing the affected feature.
